TRAVEL SMART ✈️

Travel Tips & Essentials

Simple tips to help make your journey safer, easier and more enjoyable.

📄

Keep Your Documents Safe

Keep your passport, ID, tickets and other important documents safe. Store digital copies as a backup.

💰

Manage Your Budget

Plan your travel budget before your trip. Keep some emergency money separately.

🗺️

Plan Your Route

Check your destinations and transportation options before travelling.

🧳

Pack Smart

Carry only the essentials and check the weather of your destination before packing.

📱

Stay Connected

Keep your phone charged and make sure you have access to maps and important contacts.

🛡️

Stay Safe

Be aware of your surroundings and follow local rules and safety guidelines.

🌦️

Check the Weather

Check the weather forecast before leaving so you can plan your activities and clothing.

🤝

Respect Local Culture

Learn about local customs, traditions and basic etiquette before visiting a new place.

Before You Travel ✈️

✅ Passport / ID
✅ Travel tickets
✅ Accommodation details
✅ Emergency contacts
✅ Phone & charger
✅ Basic medicines
